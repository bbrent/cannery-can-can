How it works

Each kind of animal wants one particular horn: a pitch, a tone and a pace, and some want an extra as well. The empty dots on its card show how many things it wants.

Hear their call to pick up the note and the rhythm they like. Their own voice won't tell you the tone they like.

Rehearse to try the selected horn on every animal in the wood at once. Each kind lights one dot for every thing the horn gets right, without saying which. With every dot lit they dance the can-can, and they will follow that horn. If the horn has something they hate, they run off and you learn nothing else. You get 5 rehearsals a day, and the notebook keeps every result.

When you march, the band walks the trail playing the song. An animal joins the train when a horn it loves is playing within reach of its bell. An animal that hears a sound it hates runs off, even after joining, so plan who plays in which part of the song.

The trail ends at the cannery, where every animal becomes cans of something nice. The band is paid in cans: wages for each musician, the parts in each horn, and a fee for the day. After the first day you can march again for whoever is left, or move on.
